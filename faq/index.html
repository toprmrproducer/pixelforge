Frequently asked questions

Is PixelForge really free?+

Yes. There is no account, no paywall, and no limit on how many images you convert. The tool is supported by lightweight advertising.

Do my images get uploaded anywhere?+

No. Every conversion happens locally in your browser using canvas and WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device.

What formats can I convert from?+

Anything your browser can natively display — JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, and SVG — plus HEIC/HEIF (iPhone photos) and TIFF, which are decoded on-device with a small helper library.

What formats can I convert to?+

PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, or BMP. PNG and BMP are lossless; GIF is limited to 256 colors; JPEG and WebP are lossy with an adjustable quality slider.

Can I convert multiple images at once?+

Yes. Drop or select as many files as you like — each is converted independently, so one problematic file will not stop the rest of the batch.

Why isn't RAW (CR2, NEF, ARW, DNG) supported?+

RAW camera formats require proprietary demosaicing algorithms that are not practical to run inside a browser, so they are intentionally out of scope.

Does PixelForge work on mobile?+

Yes, PixelForge runs in any modern mobile browser, including on iPhone for HEIC photos straight from your camera roll.

All conversions run locally in your browser. Nothing you upload is ever sent to a server.
